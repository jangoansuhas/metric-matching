# Counterexample decision audit (development)

Scope: the executed public Jaffle Shop seed experiments in [food revenue](experiment_food_revenue_report.md), [gross profit](experiment_gross_profit_report.md), and [cumulative revenue](experiment_cumulative_revenue_report.md), with their respective `experiment_*.py` scripts, at commit `7be2c5838dbdeca8e915d4e46db70e910753d7f6`. These are conditional DuckDB observations on reconstructed models, not human gold or a new held-out source.

## Decision rule

Fix the two definitions and a claimed substitution domain: output grain and key mapping, requested time bounds, eligible base population and filters, units/types, snapshot, and the policy for missing groups and `NULL`. Evaluate each definition **as declared** in that same request context; a declared food mask or cumulative lookback must remain part of its calculation. Compare the same keys, including presence/absence if the contract promises a row, and use `IS DISTINCT FROM` or equivalent NULL-aware value comparison rather than `<>` in a SQL `WHERE` clause. One supported key with unequal numbers, `NULL` versus a number, or a contractually significant missing group versus a present group **refutes equality at that context** and any universal equivalence claim that includes it. Two `NULL`s are not a value mismatch; an unstated `COALESCE`, an accidental mismatch in the comparison's requested population, or differently bounded requests cannot supply a witness. Matching finite values cannot prove equality on other rows or contexts.

## Classification boundary and verification

The six substantive relationship labels in [the review protocol](independent_review_protocol.md) are `direct_equivalent`, `cross_grain_equivalent`, `temporal_or_scope_variant`, `related`, `conflicting_definition`, and `non_match` (with `needs_review` for insufficient evidence). A valid mismatch rejects **unqualified equality for the witnessed substitution scope**. It does not decide whether a documented transformation, scope/time relation, shared domain, identity conflict, or unrelated quantity applies. That requires source and lineage interpretation, group/population and NULL rules, and the intended relationship; even repeated finite agreement cannot prove a cross-grain or universal identity. The food mask and cumulative operator explain their observed differences, while gross profit's dependency explains its calculation; none of these observations automatically assigns a positive class.

Read-only reruns of the scripts' pinned input checks and in-memory queries reproduced the food totals and **365 / 60,334** differing day/order counts; gross profit's **365** agreeing days, **483** both-NULL orders and scalar totals; and the two bounded compiled month SQL statements extracted from the cumulative report, agreeing with the direct control on all **12** months and differing from each other on all **12**. No report was regenerated.
