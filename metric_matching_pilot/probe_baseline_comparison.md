## Why the baselines abstain

- **AST-lineage baseline.** Normalized ASTs differ, and its full declared-measure keys differ despite the common physical relation and semantic model. Its 0.2 score comes from `same_physical_sources_only`. Both cards have `not_certified_by_compiled_bundle` numeric status and `metricflow_sql_generated_not_executed` source status; expression/field-owner traces and independent semantic verification are missing. Even if those blockers were later supplied, its `temporal_or_scope_variant` rule requires the *same* bound measure and payload with differing declared metric filters and compiled `WHERE` predicates. These pairs change the aggregate argument and have no `WHERE` difference. There is no direct derived-metric dependency for its `related` rule either. It has no branch that would classify these masks under the same observed expression pattern.
- **Constraint-aware baseline.** Its resolved measure bindings are `owner:model.jaffle_shop.order_items/order_item/drink_revenue` or `.../food_revenue` versus `.../revenue`, so `measure_binding` is `contradicted`. The declared expression and compiled SQL identity also differ; the same `sum`, `ordered_at`, source model, and absent metric filter are only partial agreement. Its source mapping is `generated_compiled`, numeric eligibility and population/context are unresolved, and its first applicable branch is `different_measures_same_model`, which has no decision assignment. Its documented filter-variant, time-variant, shared-measure, and direct-equivalence decision paths all require a shared bound measure (or, for the separate conflict path, the same metric ID). They do not classify these pairs even if the currently unknown context were independently verified.
- **Proposed review method.** Its same-model 0.2 score is weak co-location evidence. Its `filter_scope_review` requires the same declared measure and differences in both declared metric filters and generated `WHERE` predicates. Neither condition holds. It emits no mask-specific hypothesis and always abstains by contract.

The common runner rejects any non-`abstain` semantic output on this frozen packet. The stored [summary report](compiled_development_baselines_report.md) shows 171 abstentions for **each** I1 method and zero common decided pairs. Accordingly, neither baseline emits a semantic decision for either conditional-mask pair under this packet and runner. Their inability to emit a mask-specific signal is a limit of their fixed rules; it is not a judged false negative.

## Fair comparison and proof limits

The probe checks a targeted `SUM(CASE WHEN flag THEN x ELSE 0 END)` versus `SUM(x)` pattern, including owner/model/time agreement, full single-aggregate ungrouped SQL, and a flag mention in the public owner model. The I1 cards contain the declared expressions, generated SQL, and owner-model raw code used for that pattern; the probe also checks those source spans against the pinned public commit. The baselines use the same pair cards in the comparison runner, but their fixed rules do not recognize an internal aggregate mask as a filter variant. This is a comparison of **signal coverage for two development examples**, not a comparison of calibrated scores or decision quality. Each method's candidate score has its own ordinal rule; 0.2 versus 0.0 is not a common probability scale. The full 171-pair denominator and the 153-pair common-scope ranking frame should remain distinct.

The packet does not establish full upstream lineage or Boolean truth semantics for `is_drink_item` and `is_food_item`, a source-field classification rule, grouped MetricFlow behavior at aligned grain/time, base population, join cardinality, missing groups, NULL policy, units, snapshot, or runtime numeric type. Price sign would also matter for any numerical ordering assertion. The shared source model and ungrouped SUM forms support review of a structural conditional mask; they do not establish inclusion, equivalence, or a taxonomy class. There are no independent reference labels here, so no Recall@k, precision, unsafe-equivalence rate, or method-superiority claim follows. No held-out source or worksheets entered this audit.
