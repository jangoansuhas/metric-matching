## What could make an Industrial full paper credible

1. **A stable, consequential task and sample.** Several independent public projects with genuine, documented maintenance workflows and enough declared metrics to study natural decisions; a complete project/declaration/pair flow including failures and exclusions. A two-project synthetic/demo screen or a convenient declaration filter alone is not industrial validation.
2. **Independent, source-linked judgments.** Two blind qualified annotators and distinct adjudication on a frozen, prediction-free sample; agreement and unresolved prerequisites shown by project. Capture which scoped replacement, reconciliation, or review action is correct. Report sparse positives and unknowns rather than filling them with constructed controls or AI labels.
3. **A useful comparison at the same evidence budget.** Full extraction and decision coverage, strong mask-aware AST and constraint baselines, common-decided-pair unsafe-equivalence denominators, assertion counts, abstention and project-aware uncertainty. For a superiority claim, a portable rule and a prospectively specified practical margin/power analysis must accompany enough adjudicated equivalence assertions; the 20-per-method floor is a minimum analyzability condition, not proof of power. A tie should become a measured failure-mode or benchmark contribution only if the empirical investigation itself is substantial.
4. **Observed maintenance relevance.** At least a source-reproducible maintainer task showing actionable decisions and failure costs, preferably practitioner judgments or a separately measured review outcome. Do not infer time saved, adoption, downstream impact, or enterprise scale from a README, scheduled workflow, hypothetical PR text, or seed-row counterexample.

The current record has **none** of the new-cohort denominators, human labels, matched held-out results, or observed utility. The user reports a submission extension, so the earlier posted October dates are **not a binding gate for this review**. The binding constraint is a staffed, completed empirical study with a stable scope and credible practical finding. If the finite evidence and capacity gate fails, write a transparent coverage/failure report and defer a full-paper claim. Do not relabel the result as a demonstrated detector improvement or keep changing cohorts to obtain favorable outcomes.
