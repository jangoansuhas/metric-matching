# Independent progress-to-SANER gate review

**29 September 2026 — AI technical review, never human gold.** I reviewed the current outline, three bounded development experiments, the one-rule probe, and the subsequent comparison, grouped-scope, scoped-decision, and flag-lineage reports. I inspected no held-out source or practice worksheet.

**Gate: plausible, conditional path; no publishable evaluation yet.** The public, within-revision metric-maintenance task can fit a SANER Industrial full paper if it becomes a systematic, practically grounded multi-project study. The [Industrial call](https://conf.researchr.org/track/saner-2027/saner-2027-industrial-track) emphasizes industrial relevance, soundness, and empirical or practical validation. The Jaffle Shop seed experiments expose real scope, time-grain, and missing-group failure modes: food revenue differs from total revenue; cumulative revenue agrees with it ungrouped but diverges by month; and 483 orders lack an item group for a derived profit calculation. These bounded observations are neither method accuracy nor universal equivalence.

**Rubric and method decision — accept the two development outputs.** The packet defines `temporal_or_scope_variant` as a documented shared measure concept with changed time rule, filter, state, or population. Here the published descriptions call the measures total, food, and drink revenue; the expressions sum the same `product_price` on the same `order_item` model, with `CASE WHEN is_food_item/is_drink_item THEN product_price ELSE 0 END` masking contributions. An internal aggregate mask can express a food/drink scope restriction even without a metric-level `WHERE` or identical measure IDs. A `temporal_or_scope_variant` **method prediction** is therefore defensible at the explicitly ungrouped scope. The separate generated MetricFlow day queries align on `ordered_at` and support the same *conceptual* distinction at day scope, but the packet-only rule makes no day-scoped output. Full NULL/Boolean lineage is not a blanket prerequisite for this class; it matters for exact numerical reconciliation, subset ordering, substitution, or universal claims. Human adjudication remains the reference authority.

The original one-rule probe had **two conditional signals and zero semantic decisions among 171 pairs**. The separate `scoped_mask_decision.py` now returns **two ungrouped class predictions and 169 abstentions**; I reproduced both IDs and counts read-only. Its revised guard checks that each pinned description contains the base concept `revenue` and a flag-derived token. **Revise before generalizing:** this lexical test is narrow and can misread compound or negated flags (`is_not_food_item` could pass a “food revenue” description), while synonyms may cause abstention. Require unambiguous category and polarity evidence or explicitly retain narrow coverage. The baseline comparison finds the frozen AST/lineage, constraint-aware, and proposed I1 methods abstaining on these two pairs from the same cards. This is a decision-coverage difference, not a judged false negative, accuracy gain, or unsafe-equivalence result.

**Corrected fixture and lineage boundary.** The grouped report now applies staging `COALESCE` before the mart's product `LEFT JOIN`. I independently reproduced its absent-SKU row: `(price, food flag, drink flag) = (NULL, NULL, NULL)` and grouped `(revenue, food, drink) = (NULL, 0, 0)`. A matched product with NULL raw type instead has a false staged flag; an empty input emits no day group. All 365 observed seed days reconcile as `revenue = food + drink`, but that partition does not extend to unknown products or NULL prices. The flag-lineage report's pinned YAML, mart and staging spans and source hashes corroborate the two known cases, and its separate Boolean/NULL fixture agrees with my check. **Its automated lineage script is not complete:** I reproduced `Unsupported: projection_not_unique:order_items.product_price` at the direct mart projection. The bundle and checkout also use different physical relation names, so one build identity is not attested. This failure does **not** block the two bounded, source-supported conceptual class predictions; it **does** block a claim of passing end-to-end automated flag lineage or general provenance coverage.

**Paper completion gates and work to stop.** Freeze the amended rule, generic adapter, pair frame, and matched baseline evidence budget before selected held-out source is opened. Obtain nonzero decisions on multiple independent public projects, report full-frame extraction and abstention, and fund two qualified independent human judgments plus distinct adjudication. Compare unsafe equivalence assertions only on common decided pairs at matched context, with assertion counts, uncertainty, and baseline ties; scope-variant predictions alone cannot estimate that endpoint. Show a concrete maintainer workflow without claiming deployment or time saved. The outline's floor of 20 adjudicated equivalence assertions per method across two held-out projects is a planning threshold, not achieved power. Stop extra custody layers, optional LLM or drift work, constructed positives as substitutes for natural cases, and claims based on finite equal totals or AI review. If decisions remain sparse or baselines tie, report that measured limit rather than an unsupported superiority or publishability claim.
