## Decision in brief

Adopt the call to sharpen the maintenance question, use close prior work and a strong AST/SQL baseline, and make the next evaluation falsifiable. Most methodological safeguards are **already written into the contract**; the task is to execute and report them. Do not present the selected 55 GTM pairs as accuracy evidence or the proposed method as better than the constraint-aware baseline: both produced the same two conditional hypotheses, and **all 55 outputs from each method are `needs_review`**. No human gold, held-out comparison, reviewer-effort result, or industrial outcome exists in §27. Its counts measure output behavior on development data, not F1, statistical significance, safety improvement, or novelty.

## Prior-work checks and novelty boundary

| Source checked | What the primary source supports | Implication for this paper |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ConStruM**, Houming Chen, Zhe Zhang, H. V. Jagadish, *A Structure-Guided LLM Framework for Context-Aware Schema Matching* (2026 preprint), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20482). | Budgeted, query-specific context packing for LLM **column matching** after an upstream candidate shortlist. | Context aggregation or an LLM shortlist alone is prior work. Contrast input units and outputs; test whether versioned conditional evidence changes a metric-maintenance decision. Do not call it an end-to-end business metric drift system. |
| **Valentine**, Koutras et al., *Evaluating Matching Techniques for Dataset Discovery* ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.07386)); **Valentine in Action: Matching Tabular Data at Scale** ([PVLDB demonstration](https://www.vldb.org/pvldb/vol14/p2871-koutras.pdf), 2021). | A tabular **schema/column matching** experiment suite, four dataset-discovery scenarios, dataset fabrication, ranked-match evaluation, and matching methods; the later demonstration adds a GUI and holistic matching. | Good evaluation precedent and a possible column-retrieval comparator where inputs match; it does not directly adjudicate business scalar, cross-grain prerequisites, or version events. Do not misstate the two titles as one metric-matching method. |
| **GEqO**, Haynes et al., *ML-Accelerated Semantic Equivalence Detection* ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.01280), 2024 preprint). | ML-accelerated detection of semantically equivalent computations/subexpressions for analytics reuse at scale. | Generic “we detect equivalent SQL” is a weak novelty claim. Explain the extra business definition, time/grain, evidence-status, and versioned maintenance outputs; test them against a credible SQL/expression baseline. Do not imply GEqO directly decides conditional business-metric substitutability. |
| **SQLGlot**, [official API documentation](https://sqlglot.com/sqlglot.html). | A SQL parser/transpiler/optimizer with AST inspection/diff and lineage facilities, dialect and unsupported-translation limits. | Treat as an enabling component or a strong normalization baseline, not as a published metric-matching paper or semantic proof system. Pin configuration and count parse/lineage failures. |

These are overlap and scope checks, **not** evidence that the proposed novelty has been established. The outline §3.5 already phrases that novelty as a hypothesis. A result where constraint-aware or normalized SQL alternatives match the safety/recall/review frontier should narrow the contribution to a reproducible maintenance investigation or failure-mode account.
