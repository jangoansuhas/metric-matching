## Exact overlap with the proposed decision — interpretation, not source claims

The outline's proposed unit is a **business metric definition at a version**, with source/compiled evidence, grain, filters, temporal scope and possibly consumer *references*. The desired output distinguishes a potential source change from verified behavior, safe equivalence from a conditional reconciliation with outstanding prerequisites, and review/abstention. The following comparisons are **inferences from the source-confirmed tasks above**, not assertions that a paper's authors claimed a complete absence of these capabilities.

| Prior work | Overlap we must credit | Remaining decision to test, rather than assume unique |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Valentine, ICDE and PVLDB | Ranked retrieval under known gold, schema/value baselines, scenario fabrication, scalable holistic matching, and **manual verification** are prior art. Its `Recall@ground truth` is a useful precedent for a completely judged anchor universe. | Whether a ranked column match is also a safe **metric substitution or maintenance alert** after a SQL change, with grain/window/NULL prerequisites and traceable version evidence, is a different gold label. Reusing its measure or GUI review is not a contribution. |
| ConStruM | Budgeted, query-specific, structured context; differentiation of confusable alternatives by **scope, units and timeframe**; and matching between different survey waves already exist. Neither contextual LLM prompting nor temporal context alone is a novelty claim. | Does a pre/post metric implementation change lead to a correct **alert, conditional reconciliation, or abstention** with explicit unmet source-membership/rounding/NULL conditions and affected consumer **references**? The paper's forced one-column choice does not validate that decision. |
| GEqO | Detecting more than syntactic SQL equivalence, AST/logical-plan reasoning, and formal verification of supported executable fragments are established. A mere “equivalent SQL” or “AST-aware” claim overlaps directly. | A proof about supported SPJ fragments does not by itself establish identity of business populations, snapshot policy, cross-grain aggregation, displayed rounding or downstream governance. Give GEqO's verifier a fair chance on the **supported fragment**; record aggregate/unsupported cases separately and never turn a parser failure into a win. |
| CGM | Granularity/unit constraints, hard admissibility, controlled relaxation, audit logs, **abstention** and measured expert effort all predate this proposal. Its enterprise deployment is framed as handling schema drift; the reported gold evaluates record mapping. “Constraint-aware matching,” “governed mapping,” and “human-review savings” are too broad as novelty claims. | Test the narrower before/after **metric-definition maintenance action**, including whether a proposed cross-grain mapping still holds after a code/definition change. A record alignment that remains valid under a constraint is not already a gold-labeled metric-change event; that distinction needs measured utility, not wording alone. |

### Present evidence ceiling from the specified local materials

The §27 comparison gives the normalized SQL/lineage baseline, constraint-aware baseline and proposed explanation **identical 11-card/55-pair GTM evidence**. The latter two surfaced the **same two** month-to-window hypotheses; all 55 pairs in **each** method require review. The §28 SQLGlot/AST comparator made the **same 55 decisions as the constraint-aware baseline**. On the already inspected Jaffle source-YAML adapter, all three methods abstained on **253/253** pairs. No domain-human gold, held-out classification result, randomized reviewer outcome or observed consumer impact follows. GTM's MQL “window” lacks a source date predicate; bounded displayed sums cannot prove general window equivalence. The 483 zero-item Jaffle orders illustrate NULL/`COALESCE` sensitivity outside the GTM comparison: the observed discrepancies disappear after `COALESCE`, but no general cross-grain rule was verified. These are **local development observations from the outline/triage**, not findings of the cited papers.

## One falsifiable novelty claim and a fair test

**Claim to test (hypothesis):** *At a fixed review budget, a versioned, prerequisite-explicit metric explanation improves the rate of domain-human-correct maintenance decisions about changed definitions and cross-grain substitutability, subject to a prespecified ceiling on unsafe equivalence assertions, over strong SQL/lineage and constraint-aware methods given the same evidence.* A “correct decision” must be operationally labeled as **alert for a behavior-relevant change, accept a verified reconciliation, or defer/reject because a named prerequisite is unmet**; listing consumer references is provenance, not proof of actual impact. This is narrower than introducing context packing, constraints, SQL equivalence or manual review.

**Falsification design (prospective, not completed):**

1. **Freeze the unit and gold before test inspection.** Capture the dated full search-result frame and project/commit eligibility, then select unrelated held-out projects without replacing hard cases based on labels. Sample complete metric-pair and change-event anchor universes, including no-match, aliases/refactors, real filter/population changes, cross-grain candidates and missing metadata. Two independent domain humans label relationship, before/after behavior status, each reconciliation prerequisite and the correct maintenance action; adjudicate disagreements blind to method. Keep synthetic negative counterfactuals (out-of-window rows, absent groups/zero versus NULL, rounding order, grouping-set collisions, join fanout) separate from natural held-out prevalence.
2. **Equalize *information*, and compare each method at each feasible budget.** `I0`: names only, a retrieval diagnostic, never a full-context effectiveness comparator. `I1`: the identical source/compiled SQL, metadata, filters, grain and lineage packet, pair universe and candidate order for all full-context methods. `I2` (proposed prospective definition): `I1` plus the identical pre/post implementation and dated definition evidence and available consumer-reference edges. Supply `I2` to **all** methods, not just ours; compare `I1`→`I2` within each. Fix extraction rules, parsers/dialects, model/prompt if used, maximum source text/tokens, candidate `k`, evidence provenance and cost accounting on development projects. Count missing fields and parse failures for every method. A method may have different preprocessing, but cannot read extra repository source or labels. `I2` does **not** imply that consumer behavior was observed.
3. **Include the strongest applicable comparators.** At minimum: normalized SQL/AST plus lineage, the existing constraint-aware method, and the proposed method with an ablation omitting versioned prerequisites. Offer a context-matched LLM the same shortlist and evidence/token allowance if feasible; adapt CGM-style executable admissibility/abstention on metric pairs rather than comparing an irrelevant unmodified record matcher. Use Valentine-like ranking for retrieval only. Run GEqO or a formal SQL verifier on its supported SPJ subset if implementable, with unsupported aggregates and time rules separately tallied. No one-to-one outcome comparison should silently equate a forced-choice column label or formally proved SQL fragment with owner-confirmed metric substitutability.
4. **Lock endpoints and the failure rule.** Judge complete-anchor positive retrieval at fixed `k`; among reviewed items at a fixed reviewer-time or item budget, report correct actions, unsafe equivalence assertions, abstention/coverage, reviewer effort and run cost by project, with confusion counts and project-level uncertainty. State the safety ceiling and practically meaningful improvement margin **before** seeing held-out gold. The claim fails if same-input constraint/AST/LLM alternatives match or beat the proposed safety–correct-action–effort tradeoff, if adding `I2` helps comparators equally, if apparent gains come from abstaining on nearly everything, or if cases with unresolved prerequisites cannot produce a correct action at the fixed budget. Report unsupported coverage and low sample power rather than converting non-significance or an empty denominator into evidence of novelty.

**Audit conclusion:** These primary sources leave room to **investigate** a versioned metric-maintenance decision, but they already cover much of the apparent mechanism and evaluation vocabulary. The observed development tie with constraint-aware and AST comparators makes incremental usefulness an open empirical question.
