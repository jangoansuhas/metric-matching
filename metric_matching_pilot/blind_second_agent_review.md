### Methodological concerns

Aggregate equality can conceal missing rows, as the zero-subtotal Jaffle orders demonstrate. Scalar equality through a report projection should be recorded separately from row or metric-definition equivalence. The GTM and Jaffle totals above validate raw-seed transformations but do not verify the built DuckDB snapshots. Rill’s remote inputs and clock-relative time shifts prevent a pinned-code comparison from proving observed row impact or cross-view population equality.
