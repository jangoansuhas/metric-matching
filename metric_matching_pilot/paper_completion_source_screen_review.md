## Decision

**The amended screen was a justified, material advance for corpus flow; stop selection now at ranks 1 and 2.** The two agents report accessible pinned SQL/dbt projects with at least two explicit aggregate outputs each and no identified shared development family or dataset. Their lower bounds are **two** rank-1 monthly finance outputs and **three** rank-2 author outputs, not complete numeric-output denominators. Rank 1's fresh dbt parse succeeded, but the fixed adapter saw **zero declared Semantic Layer metrics and generated zero MetricFlow metric queries**. Its MetricFlow-target denominator is zero, so this is a format/coverage gap, **not a 0% compile failure rate among declared metrics**. Rank 2's fixed-adapter attempt exited before `dbt deps` or parse with `FileNotFoundError`: it assumed a root `dbt_project.yml`, whereas this pinned project places it under `transform/`. This is a project-layout failure, not a measured parse or metric-compilation failure. The first two selections leave **60 queue rows unopened** on the reported verdicts; do not open rank 3 to find a more convenient metric format.

The cross-format and layout results are a **blocker to the outline's intended compiled-provenance method comparison as currently implemented**. The fixed adapter only iterates Semantic Layer metrics and assumes a root dbt project, whereas both selected projects' evidenced outputs are SQL aggregate-query fields and rank 2 has a nested dbt project. A separate GTM *development* check found nine custom catalog entries absent from the adapter's output: its clean run failed `dbt deps` because the registry was unavailable, and a clearly separate local-package diagnostic parsed successfully but found zero Semantic Layer metrics. These are distinct failure stages; none justifies claiming failed MetricFlow compilation across the source-defined outputs. Without a source-linked SQL/catalog inventory and an honest same-evidence comparator, selected projects cannot supply the proposed compiled I1 cards merely by running this adapter. Preserve the fixed adapter as a measured baseline coverage result; any bridge is a new, separately versioned instrument, not a silent repair of the frozen run.

The screen materially advances *feasibility* and exposes a maintenance-tool coverage problem, but it does not complete an Industrial paper. Rank 1 describes itself as a synthetic portfolio/demonstration, with no observed deployment and no license file found; rank 2 documents a GitHub-PR pipeline and scheduled workflow with an MIT software license, but actual hosted operation and practitioner use are unverified. Neither should be replaced on those grounds under the eligibility rule. Source counts and compile coverage alone cannot support relationship accuracy, incremental safety, reviewer utility, or superiority. The official [SANER 2027 Industrial Track call](https://conf.researchr.org/track/saner-2027/saner-2027-industrial-track) asks full papers for systematic investigation of industrially relevant problems and evaluates empirical/practical validation. A two-project screen without human decisions or a substantial, source-supported maintenance finding remains a feasibility result, not a ready full paper.

The amendment's internal chronology records it before affected source access and keeps the 62-row hash order and pinned commits; the audit cannot independently attest the prior non-access assertion. The fixed order reduces selection by attractive examples. **It does not eliminate bias:** a screener can see metric names, filters, SQL shapes, and apparent pairs while determining eligibility. Ambiguous choices about what counts as a scalar output, whether two repositories share a dataset, or whether a build failure disqualifies a project could become outcome-dependent. The page-one search frame also limits generalization; the screened prefix is not a random sample of industrial deployments or an eligibility census of all 62 hits.

Keep the screen operationally separate from method development and annotation. Log inspected files, exact source spans, inclusion/unknown reasons, independence evidence, failures, commit/dependency identity, and tool versions in a dated append-only ledger. Apply the prewritten output rule consistently: at least two inspectable declared or reproducibly identified aggregate numeric scalar outputs at the pinned revision; raw numeric columns alone do not qualify; alternative explicit scopes count, arbitrary runtime groupings do not. Carry unsupported and unresolved outputs in coverage denominators. Quarantine unresolved ancestry rather than guessing; do not replace a selected project for sparse positives, difficult syntax, missing local rows, or unsuccessful parsing/SQL generation. Report the fixed adapter's parse, metric SQL generation, and verified expression-link coverage as distinct stages; generated model SQL or a successful parse is not proof of a compiled metric expression or runtime behavior. Keep any incidental pair impression out of the ledger and subsequent rule tuning. Record a violation if this boundary is crossed rather than calling the affected result clean held-out evidence.
