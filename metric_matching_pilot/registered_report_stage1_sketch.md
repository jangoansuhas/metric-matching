## Structured abstract (200 words)

**Background:** Versioned SQL and dbt repositories expose metric names, formulas, lineage, and downstream references, yet a matching expression or observed total may conceal differences in grain, time, population, NULL behavior, or rounding. Maintainers need evidence for an alert, a conditional reconciliation, or abstention.

**Objective:** We will investigate whether source-linked, version-aware explanations improve decisions about scalar metric relationships and adjacent-version changes, and identify where public evidence cannot support a decision. The study is exploratory; comparative outcomes remain open.

**Method:** Before inspecting held-out source, we will freeze a dated search-result frame, select up to four unrelated eligible projects by the specified hash rule, and pin commits. Two independent domain-capable humans will judge sampled pairs and events; a third will adjudicate disputes. Complete, target-specific anchor universes will support exact retrieval recall where feasible. At matched input and review budgets, we will compare names, normalized SQL and lineage, an AST and constraint-aware baseline, and conditional explanations; a context-matched LLM is optional if feasible. We will report unsafe assertions, abstention, source coverage, retrieval, reviewer effort, cost, and project-level uncertainty.

**Expected contribution:** A reproducible account of when versioned evidence supports metric-maintenance decisions, including negative or inconclusive findings without treating development examples as human gold, and retaining project-level limitations and open questions.

## Research questions and claim boundary

1. **RQ1 (evidence and feasibility):** Across prospectively selected public projects, which scalar metric relationships and adjacent-version behaviors can domain humans establish from pinned source and reproducible checks, and which grain, time, population, join, missing-row/NULL, units, or rounding gaps force `needs_review`?
2. **RQ2 (maintenance decision):** At a fixed review budget, do versioned conditional explanations reduce unsafe equivalence assertions or improve correct maintenance decisions beyond normalized SQL/lineage and constraint-aware methods?
3. **RQ3 (discovery and work):** For completely judged within-project anchor universes, how do methods compare in discovery of direct and established conditional cross-grain positives, abstention/coverage, and the human time and cost needed to reach a correct decision?

The unit is a **numeric scalar output at a recorded grain, period, population, and comparison scope**, or one linked scalar across adjacent parent-child commits. A field alias does not establish whole-row equality. Conditional equivalence at a different scope is a second claim with an explicit transformation and verified prerequisites, not an upgrade of the native-scope label. Source-linked consumer references indicate potential exposure; actual changed consumer values and remediation require separate observations. The 55 selected GTM development pairs, Jaffle checks, and portable YAML inventory inform method design only: every GTM output needed review, the proposed decision method found no new conditional candidates beyond the constraint-aware or AST comparator, and no held-out, independent human, reviewer-effort, or industrial result exists (outline §§27–28).

## Prospective sample and independent judgment

1. **Freeze before test source:** The precommitted `dbt metric`, `dbt semantic model`, and `rill metrics` metadata search now has a dated, hashed complete capture of 65 ranked occurrences and 62 pinned distinct repositories (`heldout_search_capture/attempt-20260928T020324Z-1c3dd1fe/manifest.json`). The raw pages, response hashes, default-branch HEADs and failed earlier attempts are retained. This frame is **metadata only** and non-atomic across requests. Before test source, fix the generic method, freeze the remaining eligibility/selection and run settings, then apply the precommitted exclusion and hash rule transparently with all rejections recorded. Version any amendment before affected source or outputs are inspected.
2. **Select projects and units:** Exclude inspected Jaffle, Sidemantic, GTM, and Rill projects and derivatives. Apply source-independent eligibility, then source inventory for accessible versioned SQL/dbt definitions and at least two numeric scalars; record every rejection and unsupported definition. Select four unrelated pair-eligible projects by lexicographic SHA-256 of `owner/repo@commit` from the frozen frame (or report fewer), pin commits and material dependencies, and retain projects lacking an event. Enumerate all eligible within-project unordered pairs before drawing up to **25 pairs per project** from frozen lexical, shared-lineage, and hash-random strata; record overlap and inclusion probabilities where known. Enumerate adjacent events in the preceding **12 months / 100 most recent commits**, then draw up to **10 events per project**, including unchanged controls. Never replace a project or top up positives based on labels, parser support, or successful builds.
3. **Judge separately from predictions:** Freeze IDs, scopes, packets, rubric, reviewer timeboxes and costs on development material. Two distinct domain-capable humans receive identical pinned source-linked packets with system predictions, prior labels, and each other's submissions hidden. Each records the six-class primary relation or null, `sufficient`/`needs_review`, evidence refs, rationale, confidence, missing evidence, and independent direct/conditional target screens. For conditional claims, record transformation and each source mapping, additivity, hierarchy, join, missing-group/NULL, filter, time/snapshot, unit and rounding prerequisite as verified, inapplicable with reason, assumed, unknown, or contradicted. Events separately distinguish behavior preserving/changing from source-level potential alerts and observed consumer effects. Lock both originals; a third distinct domain-aware human adjudicates scope, label, status or prerequisite disagreements and audits a prespecified fraction of agreements. Unresolved records stay unscored, with counts and reasons. Validate record schema and independently audit blinding, provenance and completeness.
4. **Exhaust retrieval anchors:** Freeze a feasible hash-selected anchor quota and budget before test inspection. For each selected anchor, list **every other eligible scalar** at that pinned version, including extraction failures; have both humans screen every anchor-candidate incidence and adjudicate by target. Deduplicate symmetric annotation work while preserving each anchor's `N-1` denominator. If the predeclared budget is exceeded, reduce anchor *count* by the frozen fallback before labels; never prune candidates. A target with any unresolved decisive incidence makes that anchor unscored for **exact** Recall@k. Report judged-pool recall under that name if exhaustive gold is infeasible.

   **Feasibility arithmetic:** For `a` anchors among `N` eligible metrics in one project, the distinct unordered anchor-candidate tasks are `a(N-1) - a(a-1)/2`. As development-size examples only, `a=3` yields **27 tasks** for the 11-card GTM inventory and **63 tasks** for the 23-metric Jaffle inventory. Assuming **10 active minutes per judgment per human**, two independent judgments alone cost `27 × 2 × 10 = 540` minutes (9 hours) or `63 × 2 × 10 = 1,260` minutes (21 hours), before adjudication, packet/QA work and sampled pairs outside the anchor union. These are planning assumptions, not observed reviewer times or held-out sizes. If exhaustive work remains unaffordable after the frozen anchor-count fallback, the planned endpoint is fixed-pair classification/unsafe-assertion and abstention analysis on resolved sampled pairs, with **judged-pool Recall@k explicitly labeled non-exact**, coverage and unresolved counts; no exact Recall@k claim follows.

## Planned analyses and decision rules

- **Inputs and comparators:** Freeze extractor, AST dialect/version, methods, thresholds, prompts if any, candidate limits, run budgets, input matrix, output schema and primary operating point on development projects. Report `I0` name-only retrieval separately. Give normalized SQL/lineage, pinned AST normalization, practical constraint-aware and proposed methods the **same `I1` source/compiled packet and checks** for pair decisions; use matching `I2` parent-child diff/dependency/declared-consumer context for version decisions. Add a context-matched LLM only if the same evidence and a fixed prompt/model/budget can be supplied. Parser and extractor failures count against coverage, not as exclusions. Ablate version context and conditional grain/NULL/time evidence.
- **RQ1:** Report project-level class and event counts, unsupported/unknown-gold rates, prerequisites that block a decision, human agreement by relationship/status/conditional target/event, third-review frequency, and source/build coverage. Classify changes as behavior changing or preserving only with sufficient evidence; disclose domain limits of finite row checks. Do not estimate population prevalence from enriched strata without justified inclusion weights.
- **RQ2:** On the same fixed adjudicated sampled pairs, show six-class confusion counts, established versus unresolved conditional claims, unsafe **unqualified or prerequisite-omitting** equivalence assertions per issued assertion and opportunity, correct alert/conditional/abstain decisions, missed positives and coverage. Keep unknown-gold cases in a separate audit tally. Compare end-to-end pipelines at a frozen alert/review budget, counting unretrieved positives as misses. For events, report potential change and verified behavior or consumer effects separately. A proposed-method advantage is conditional on a predeclared practical margin and no material loss in positive discovery or coverage; strong comparators matching the frontier or ablations leaving decisions unchanged narrow the contribution. An explanatory negative result remains informative.
- **RQ3:** At predeclared `k=1,5,10` (capped at `N-1`), compute exact Recall@k **only** for fully resolved target-specific universes, separately for direct and conditional cross-grain positives; state whether conditional retrieval must propose a transformation. Give positive-pair and per-project rates, positive/zero-positive and unscored-anchor counts, missing rankings as misses, and project-level uncertainty or leave-one-project-out sensitivity. Report per-class F1 only where denominators and abstention treatment are interpretable. Log human active minutes, packet/QA and adjudication effort, frozen rates and cost. A separate blinded, randomized, counterbalanced explanation-versus-baseline reviewer task, with reviewers distinct from gold adjudicators, tests decision correctness and effort; do not infer effort benefit from explanation text alone.

## Risks, gates, and Stage 1 type

**Before submission or any held-out opening:** (i) make the generic compiled-source extractor portable beyond the GTM packet, pin and freeze fair AST/constraint baselines, exercise development counterfactuals for out-of-range dates, missing groups/NULL/zero, join fanout, grouping levels and rounding, and require `needs_review` where prerequisites remain unknown; (ii) lock eligibility, ID/scope rules, strata/anchor quotas, time budget, target screens, primary endpoint and practical margin, and human recruitment; (iii) retain the independently verified complete metadata search frame and hash the final methods/protocol addendum. The existing development snapshot freeze is not a held-out preregistration. No new held-out source is opened for this sketch.

**Feasibility after selection:** Report fewer than four eligible independent projects, unavailable source or builds, missing events, few natural positives, unjudged anchor incidences, and reviewer attrition without outcome-based replacement. If exact retrieval gold or the separate reviewer study is unaffordable, limit the respective claim to judged-pool discovery or descriptive work logs. If positives are too rare or project-level uncertainty too wide, present a scoped failure-mode investigation instead of a superiority or safety estimate. Public source and human review suffice for this study; industrial benefit needs a separately permissioned sample and observed owner decisions.

**Registration choice:** Submit the above as **exploratory** if the method or natural-positive feasibility remains uncertain; the comparison remains frozen but carries no automatic IPA or superiority claim. A **confirmatory** Stage 1 would require, *before submission and before test outcomes*, an explicit directional hypothesis, primary estimand and fixed practical margin at the operating point, outcome-neutral controls and quality checks, sample/power or feasibility justification, and pass/fail analysis including abstentions. Document amendments and deviations; never relabel exploratory findings as preregistered confirmation.
