After the selected projects and numeric IDs are frozen, enumerate **all unordered within-project pairs** at each pinned revision, including definition-level extraction failures. Draw pairs in repeating **lexical, lineage, hash-random** slots, taking the highest-ranked as-yet-unselected pair from that stratum; an empty slot stays empty. Lexical order is descending score, then pair hash; lineage and random order use pair hash. Stop after **8 slots per project** in the core (at most 16 pairs total) or **12 slots per project** in the staffed expansion (at most 48). Expansion continues slots 9–12 without redrawing the core.
